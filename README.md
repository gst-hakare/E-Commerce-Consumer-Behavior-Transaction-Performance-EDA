# 📊 E-Commerce-Consumer-Behavior-Transaction-Performance-EDA
 

---

## 📌 Project Overview
* **Project Title:** E-Commerce Consumer Behavior & Sales Performance Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Programming Language:** Python
* **Libraries:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
* **Environment:** Jupyter Notebook

---

## 🎯 Objective
* Perform an end-to-end audit and cleaning of 10,000+ raw e-commerce transaction records by removing duplicate entries, handling missing values, standardizing datetime formats, and correcting data entry errors.
* Identify customer demographic patterns, spending behavior, and top revenue-driving product categories through univariate, bivariate, and multivariate analysis.
* Analyze the operational impact of delivery turnaround times on customer satisfaction ratings to provide actionable, data-backed business insights.

---

## 📈 Conclusion & Key Findings
* **Demographics & Demand:** The primary customer base is aged between 25 and 45 years (median: 36 years), with `Fashion & Apparel` and `Electronics` generating more than 52% of total transaction volume.
* **Payment Trends:** Digital transactions dominate the platform, with `Credit Card` (34.7%) and `Debit Card` (22.7%) accounting for over 57% of all purchases.
* **Logistics & Ratings:** Delivery times within 1–5 days maintain the highest satisfaction scores (average rating ≥ 3.3/5.0), whereas delivery delays exceeding 10 days cause a noticeable decline in customer ratings (~3.16/5.0).
* **Business Takeaway:** Revenue can be maximized by targeting promotions to working professionals (ages 25–45) in top categories and maintaining strict delivery under 5 days to safeguard customer retention and satisfaction.
