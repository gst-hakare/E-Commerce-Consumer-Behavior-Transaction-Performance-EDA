## 🎯 Objective
* Perform an end-to-end audit and cleaning of 10,000+ raw e-commerce transaction records by removing duplicate entries, handling missing values, standardizing datetime formats, and correcting data entry errors[cite: 2, 4].
* Identify customer demographic patterns, spending behavior, and top revenue-driving product categories through univariate, bivariate, and multivariate analysis[cite: 2, 4].
* Analyze the operational impact of delivery turnaround times on customer satisfaction ratings to provide actionable, data-backed business insights[cite: 2, 4].

---

## 📈 Conclusion & Key Findings
* **Demographics & Demand:** The primary customer base is aged between 25 and 45 years (median: 36 years), with `Fashion & Apparel` and `Electronics` generating more than 52% of total transaction volume[cite: 4].
* **Payment Trends:** Digital transactions dominate the platform, with `Credit Card` (34.7%) and `Debit Card` (22.7%) accounting for over 57% of all purchases[cite: 4].
* **Logistics & Ratings:** Delivery times within 1–5 days maintain the highest satisfaction scores (average rating ≥ 3.3/5.0), whereas delivery delays exceeding 10 days cause a noticeable decline in customer ratings (~3.16/5.0)[cite: 4].
* **Business Takeaway:** Revenue can be maximized by targeting promotions to working professionals (ages 25–45) in top categories and maintaining strict delivery SLAs under 5 days to safeguard customer retention and satisfaction[cite: 4].
